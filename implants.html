Frequently Asked Questions

Am I suitable for dental implants?

Most adults in good general health with adequate jawbone density are ideal candidates. A digital x-ray evaluation determines your exact bone volume.

Is the implant placement painful?

No. Implant placement is performed under gentle local anesthesia and is generally reported by patients to be as comfortable as a routine filling.

How long do dental implants last?

With routine brushing, flossing, and professional checkups, titanium dental implants have an industry success rate exceeding 95% and can last a lifetime.
