Dental Implant vs. Traditional Bridge

Restorative Feature Fixed Dental Implant Traditional Tooth-Supported Bridge
Neighbor Tooth Preservation 100% Untouched (Zero shaving or grinding of healthy neighbors) Damages 2 Healthy Teeth (Enamel is permanently ground down)
Jawbone Preservation Stimulates Bone (Prevents facial bone shrinkage permanently) Bone Deteriorates (Bone underneath gap continues to melt away)
Longevity & Durability Lifetime Solution (Engineered to last with routine oral care) 7–12 Years Average (Requires replacement when anchors decay)
Daily Oral Hygiene Normal Flossing (Clean between teeth just like natural enamel) Difficult (Requires special threaders to floss beneath fused teeth)
Recurrent Cavity Risk Zero (Medical titanium and zirconia cannot develop cavities) High (Decay often starts under capped supporting teeth)

Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the implant placement painful?

No. The entire procedure is performed under computerized local anesthesia. Patients routinely report feeling only gentle vibration and mild pressure. Postoperative discomfort is mild and easily managed with standard oral medication.

What is included in the ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 estimate?

Our single-tooth estimate includes the 3D surgical planning scan, the biocompatible medical titanium root fixture, the precision-milled titanium abutment connector, and the final custom monolithic zirconia crown.

How long does the entire process take?

The surgical placement takes only 45–60 minutes. Following placement, a 3-month healing period allows the titanium post to naturally bond with your bone (osseointegration) before the permanent crown is attached.

Do you offer flexible staged payments?

Yes. Payments are split across milestones: part during surgical fixture placement, and the remainder upon final crown delivery.
