Frequently Asked Questions

Am I suitable for dental implants?

Most adults in good general health with adequate jawbone density are ideal candidates. A digital clinical assessment and radiographic evaluation by Dr. Meghna Chauhan determines your exact suitability.

Is the implant placement painful?

No. Placement is performed under gentle, precise local anesthesia and is routinely reported by patients to be as comfortable as a standard filling.

How long do dental implants last?

With routine brushing, flossing, and professional checkups, medical titanium implants have an industry success rate exceeding 95% and can last a lifetime.
