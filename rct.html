Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Root Canal hurt at Apex Dental?

No. With computerized localized anesthesia and gentle microscopic instrumentation, a root canal eliminates severe toothache rather than causing pain. Most patients experience instantaneous relief and report feeling only gentle vibration.

Can an RCT really be finished in a single sitting?

Yes. Over 80% of uncomplicated nerve infections can be completely cleansed, disinfected, and hermetically sealed in a single 45-minute appointment. Severe periapical abscesses may receive an antibacterial medicament and a brief second visit.

Do I always need a crown after an RCT?

A protective ceramic or zirconia crown is strongly recommended for posterior molar and premolar teeth. Once the nerve and blood vessels are removed, the tooth can become brittle under heavy chewing forces; a crown protects it from splitting.

What is the success rate of microscopic RCT?

When performed by an endodontic specialist using surgical magnification and 3D hermetic sealing, root canal therapy has a clinical success rate exceeding 95% and can preserve your natural tooth for decades.
