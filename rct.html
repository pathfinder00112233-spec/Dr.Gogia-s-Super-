Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Root Canal hurt at Apex Dental?

No. With localized computer-controlled anesthesia and gentle clinical technique, root canal treatment eliminates pain rather than causing it. Most patients experience instant pain relief.

How many appointments are required?

Depending on the severity of the infection, many root canals are completed in a single sitting or two comfortable visits.

Do I always need a crown after an RCT?

A crown is highly recommended for posterior chewing teeth to reinforce the tooth structure and prevent fractures under bite pressure.
