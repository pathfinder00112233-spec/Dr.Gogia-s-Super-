Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Root Canal hurt at Radiance Dental?

No. With localized modern anesthesia and Dr. Meghna Chauhan's gentle, gentle technique, root canal treatment eliminates pain rather than causing it. Most patients experience instant pain relief.

How many appointments are required?

Depending on the severity of the infection, many root canal treatments are completed comfortably in a single sitting or two scheduled visits.

Do I always need a crown after an RCT?

A crown is strongly advised for back chewing teeth to reinforce the natural structure and protect the tooth from biting fractures.
