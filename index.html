Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful to place?

No. The procedure is completed under local anesthesia. Most patients report feeling only gentle vibration and mild pressure, with postoperative recovery feeling similar to a simple filling.

Do you give written cost estimates before treatment begins?

Yes. Following your 3D scan and clinical exam, you will receive a transparent written breakdown of all available materials and exact pricing before any treatment starts.

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Minor adjustments can take 3 to 6 months, while full alignment typically takes between 8 and 14 months depending on tooth movement requirements.

What should I do in case of a sudden dental emergency?

We reserve dedicated slots daily for acute emergencies, severe toothaches, and fractured crowns. Select "Urgent Dental Pain Relief" in the booking desk or call our reception directly for immediate same-day priority.

What sedation or comfort options do you offer anxious patients?

We specialize in gentle, low-stress dentistry. We provide topical numbing gel before every injection, computer-controlled local anesthesia, and calming chairside breaks whenever requested.

Do you offer flexible EMI or staged payment options?

Yes. For multi-stage treatments such as dental implants, full smile makeovers, and clear aligners, payments are divided across visits as your clinical milestones progress. Zero-cost financing options are also available.

Are both specialists available on weekends?

Yes. Our clinic maintains full consulting and procedure hours on both Saturdays and Sundays so patients can schedule appointments without missing weekday work or school.
