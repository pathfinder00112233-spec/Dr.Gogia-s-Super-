Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful to place?

No. The procedure is completed under local anesthesia. Most patients report feeling only gentle vibration and mild pressure, with postoperative recovery feeling similar to a simple filling.

Do you give written cost estimates before treatment begins?

Yes. Following your 3D scan and clinical exam, you will receive a transparent written breakdown of all available materials and exact pricing before any treatment starts.

What is included in a dental implant estimate?

Our implant estimate includes the initial 3D surgical planning scan, the biocompatible medical titanium root fixture, the precision-milled titanium abutment connector, and the final monolithic zirconia crown.

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Minor adjustments can take 3 to 6 months, while full alignment typically takes between 8 and 14 months depending on tooth movement requirements.

What should I do in case of a sudden dental emergency?

We reserve dedicated slots daily for acute emergencies, severe toothaches, and fractured crowns. Select "Urgent Dental Pain Relief" in the booking desk or call our reception directly for same-day priority.

Do you offer flexible staged payment options?

Yes. For multi-stage treatments such as dental implants and clear aligners, payments are divided across visits as your clinical milestones progress.

Are both specialists available on weekends?

Yes. Our clinic maintains consulting and procedure hours on Saturdays (10:00 AM – 7:30 PM) and Sundays (10:00 AM – 2:00 PM).
