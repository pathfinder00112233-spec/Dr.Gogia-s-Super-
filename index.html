Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Will porcelain veneers cause tooth sensitivity?

No. Dr. Srishti uses micro-conservative preparation techniques that stay strictly within the enamel layer, ensuring complete bond seal and 0 post-procedure sensitivity.

Can I preview my new smile before the veneers are made?

Yes. Through Digital Smile Design (DSD), we fabricate a clinical aesthetic mockup in your mouth so you can evaluate the tooth shape, symmetry, and shade match before final ceramic milling.

What makes computer-guided implants better?

Computer-guided 3D mapping uses low-dose digital tomographic scans to plan the exact depth and angle of the titanium post, minimizing surgical time, preserving natural bone, and eliminating guessing.

How many visits are required for porcelain veneers?

Typically just 2 visits across 5 to 7 days. The first visit covers digital scanning, preparation, and aesthetic temporaries. The second visit permanently bonds your custom laboratory-crafted porcelain veneers.

Do you offer flexible installment options (EMIs)?

Yes. Staged payment plans and convenient 0% interest EMIs are available for full-mouth aesthetic makeovers and multi-unit dental implants.
