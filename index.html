Frequently Asked Questions
Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful at Dr. Gogia's?

No. Dr. Pradeep Gogia holds an MDS in Endodontics and specializes in microscopic, painless root canal therapy. Using motorized rotary files and localized anesthesia, 95% of patients report no pain during the procedure.

Which branch should I visit — Model Town or Sector-2?

Both branches are fully equipped with digital imaging and sterilization facilities. You can choose whichever location is closest to your residence via our intake form above.

Are both branches open on weekends?

Yes! We understand dental pain doesn't wait for weekdays. Both our Model Town and Sector-2 clinics operate on Saturdays and Sundays.
