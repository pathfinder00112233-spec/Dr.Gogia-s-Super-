Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful to place?

No. Surgical procedures are carried out under local computer-controlled anesthesia. Most patients report mild pressure and return to normal routines the next day.

Do you provide written price commitments before starting?

Yes. Following your digital 3D examination, you receive a detailed, printed estimate covering the fixture, titanium abutment, and final zirconia crown with no unexpected add-ons.

What is included in clear aligner treatment?

Treatment covers the initial digital scan, customized simulation, complete series of aligner trays, monthly review appointments, and initial retainers.
