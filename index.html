Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful at Dr. Gogia's?

No. Dr. Pradeep Gogia holds an MDS in Endodontics and specializes in microscopic, painless root canal therapy. Using motorized rotary files and localized anesthesia, procedures are comfortable and relieve acute pain.

Do you provide written cost estimates prior to procedures?

Yes. Following a thorough clinical and radiographic examination, all available treatment choices, materials, and exact costs are explained before beginning any procedure.

Are both doctors available for consultations?

Yes. Dr. Pradeep Gogia (MDS) oversees microscopic root canal therapy and aesthetic tooth restoration, while Dr. Shilpa Gogia (BDS) directs family dentistry, pediatric exams, and preventive oral care.

Is the clinic open on weekends?

Yes. We understand dental emergencies can happen anytime. Our Model Town clinic operates with regular consulting hours on both Saturdays and Sundays.
