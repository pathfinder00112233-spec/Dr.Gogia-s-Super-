Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours a day must I wear clear aligners?

To achieve optimal and timely tooth movement, aligners must be worn 20 to 22 hours daily. You only take them off for eating, hot beverages, and brushing.

How long does clear aligner treatment take in Rohtak?

Depending on whether you have minor spacing or complex crowding, treatment usually ranges from 6 to 14 months. A digital 3D scan gives your exact schedule.

Is clear aligner treatment painful?

No. You will feel a mild, reassuring tightness for the first 24–48 hours of wearing a new tray, showing that your teeth are gently shifting into place.
