Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours a day must I wear my aligners?

To achieve optimal tooth movement on schedule, aligners must be worn for 20 to 22 hours per day. You remove them solely for eating meals, drinking hot beverages, and brushing your teeth.

Is clear aligner treatment painful?

No. You will feel a mild, reassuring pressure for the first 24 to 48 hours of wearing a new tray, which indicates that your teeth are gently shifting into position. There are no sharp metal brackets to cut your gums or cheeks.

Can aligners fix complex crowding or bite issues?

Yes. Modern digital aligners utilize custom tooth attachments and precision biomechanics to address rotations, deep bites, overbites, and severe crowding effectively.

Do you offer flexible staged payments for aligners?

Yes. Orthodontic payments are conveniently spread across your appointment milestones throughout active treatment.
