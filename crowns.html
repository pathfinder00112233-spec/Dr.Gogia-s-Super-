Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Does getting a dental crown hurt?

No. The tooth preparation procedure is performed under gentle, computerized local anesthesia so the tooth is 100% numbed. Patients feel only slight vibration during shaping.

How many appointments does a crown take?

Typically just 2 visits. Visit 1 involves conservative preparation and digital optical scanning (you leave with a comfortable temporary crown). Visit 2 (3–5 days later) is for permanent seating and bite verification.

What is the difference between a crown and a veneer?

A crown covers the entire 360-degree circumference of a weakened tooth to restore structural strength (often after a root canal or major fracture). A veneer covers only the visible front surface of healthy teeth to correct cosmetic shade, chips, or alignment.

How long will a monolithic zirconia crown last?

With regular brushing, daily flossing, and biannual checkups, monolithic zirconia restorations easily last 15 to 20+ years, and often a lifetime.
